Lyrics scans a music folder you choose with Android's Storage Access Framework. It can look up lyrics from the sources you enable, compare results, and store a selected result in a standard audio lyrics field. Audio files stay on your device. Online sources receive track metadata only after you opt in.

Automatic writing starts off. Before a manual write, you can review, edit, and adjust timing. The app writes a separate tagged candidate, checks its standard lyrics field, and retains a restorable backup of the original. A Needs attention screen lists recovery files.

Supported writing formats are MP3, FLAC, M4A, audio MP4, and WebM. Other formats are visible but cannot be tagged yet. No analytics, Google Play Services, or proprietary SDKs are included.
